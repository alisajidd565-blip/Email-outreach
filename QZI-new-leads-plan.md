## 3. Three tracks

### Track A — Gulf and Arabian houses (PRIMARY, new)

**25 uncontacted rows already in your list.** This is the single best untested opportunity
and here is the reasoning:

1. **Oud and bakhoor are smoke.** Smoke is the one product attribute a still photograph
   physically cannot show. This is the strongest product-to-service argument available in
   the entire fragrance market, and it is a genuine argument, not a sales line.
2. **Your location becomes an advantage instead of a liability.** Lahore to Dubai is a one
   hour time difference. You work the same hours, you share cultural context, and the
   "where are you located" question that killed the Perfume Shoppe thread is a non-issue
   in the Gulf.
3. **Payment is easier.** Gulf businesses routinely pay contractors in Pakistan and India.
   Wise and Payoneer both work. No "wire money to a stranger overseas" hesitation.
4. **Volume need.** Houses like Rasasi, Ard Al Zaafaran, Emper, Fragrance World and Paris
   Corner release large catalogues. Brands with many SKUs and constant launches need
   constant creative. That is a retainer shape, not a one-off shape.
5. **Timing.** The Gulf fragrance calendar peaks around Ramadan and Eid in early 2027.
   Brands commission that creative in November and December. Building the relationship
   now is correctly timed, not early.

**Caveat, stated honestly:** the larger houses may have in-house creative teams, and I
have no evidence either way. Treat Track A as a **test of 25**, not a certainty. If the
big houses do not bite, the premium end (Lootah, Taif Al Emarat, Widian, Oud Elite,
Fragrance Du Bois) is the better target inside the same segment.

## 5. Daily workflow, time boxed

**About 2.5 hours a day. Do it in this order.**

| Block | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Answer every reply from yesterday. Replies come before everything |
| 2 | 60 min | Research 12 to 15 prospects. Founder name, live launch, current video presence, one verified hook |
| 3 | 40 min | Write and send those 12 to 15. One link, matched to segment |
| 4 | 15 min | Send scheduled follow ups (day 4, 10, 18) |
| 5 | 15 min | Update `QZI-tracker-v2.csv` |

**Spec ads are rationed.** At 3 to 4 hours each you cannot make them speculatively:
- Anyone who **replies** gets one within 48 hours. Always. No exceptions.
- Plus **two per week** for the highest value cold targets only.

That is roughly 10 to 14 hours a week on spec work, which is the correct ceiling.

### Research: 4 minutes per prospect, not 15

1. Open the site. What did they launch most recently? (30s)
2. Open their Instagram. Stills or video? Last post date? (60s)
3. Find a named human. About page, footer, Instagram bio, LinkedIn. (60s)
4. Meta Ad Library search on the brand name. Running ads = has budget. (30s)
5. Write the one hook sentence into the tracker. (60s)

If you cannot find a named person or a real hook in four minutes, **skip the prospect.**
There are 117 uncontacted rows and more to source. Do not force a weak one.

## 8. What success looks like in 30 days

Do not measure sends. Measure these:

| Metric | Target | Meaning if missed |
|---|---|---|
| Emails sent | ~240 | Consistency problem |
| Replies | 5 to 15 | Below 3: deliverability or targeting. Above 15: scale it |
| Spec ads made | 8 to 12 | Not converting replies fast enough |
| Paying clients | 2 to 3 | Closing problem, re-read the Closing Rules |
| Testimonials | 2 | Not asking on delivery |

These are targets, not predictions. Nobody can promise a reply rate, and the honest
position is that Track A is untested.
