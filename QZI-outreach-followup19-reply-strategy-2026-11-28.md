# QZI follow-up 19: reply-focused follow-ups to the 26 Nov batches (2026-11-28)

Why: after 2 days, batches 15/16/17 (26 Nov) got autoresponders only, plus one genuine
"not interested" (Maison Nikoui). Diagnosis: the Gmail connector rewrites every URL into a
google.com/url?q=... redirect, so plain-text emails showed long ugly redirect links (looks
spammy, kills clicks). The first emails were also long, with several asks.

## New strategy (use for all follow-ups from now on)
- Reply IN THE SAME THREAD (replyThreadId), subject "Re: <original>".
- HTML email; links are labelled anchors (clean visible text), never raw URLs.
- Under 80 words. ONE low-friction question: "Which one feels closest to how you would want
  <product> to look on screen? Just reply with its name." The reply is a single word.
- Offer: "I will sketch a short concept for <brand>, no obligation." No price, no free-ad pitch.
- Three diverse films per email. Every email has one real client film (Cafe Kiss for KIERIN NYC, or
  Oud Epiphany for Lord of the Beards) plus two from The Beast / Reborn / The Apex (Maison Yusif) /
  Bad Influence (4K spec). Rotated across 10 combinations.
- Personal first name where the address is personal (Geoff, Daniel, Fanny, Travis, Sam, Dan,
  Andreea, Stéphanie).
- MARINE (Bennett Winch demo) NOT used as a portfolio piece.
