# "MARINE": 25-second demo film for Bennett Winch (Marine Blue Suede Weekender)

## 1. Research summary (2026-11-27)

**Brand**
- Bennett Winch: luxury bags handmade in England. Founded in London in 2014 by **Robin Bennett and Robin Winch**,
  two friends from unconnected industries. Creative director: **Rupert Shreeve**.
- Stores: Savile Row, Duke Street St James's, NYC. Lifetime Promise (repairs for life).
- Credibility: the S.C Holdall appears in **No Time To Die** (Bond's tuxedo carrier; Daniel Craig reportedly found the brand organically).
  24 Hours of Le Mans collaboration in British Racing Green (helmet bag co-designed with former Le Mans winners Harry Tincknell and Alex Lynn).
- Robin Bennett calls the **Weekender** "the iconic silhouette... our signature piece".

**Brand voice (their own words)**
- "Tools of adventure for the great escape artists. Those discerning few who shed all surplus and step beyond the grid in search of gold."
- "To the rugged and resolute." "Suit the adventure. From rivers to roads, deserts to dunes."
- "Create without compromise." "Heirloom quality." "Patina, the mark of good pursuit." "Built for a kicking." "The rough with the smooth."
- Cargo Range "designed and tested in the field by three well-travelled fathers".
  Tone: understated, literate, dry British, adventurous, never loud. No hype words.

**Their films (bennettwinch.com/pages/film, Vimeo)**
- Le Mans (71 s, 2024); Suit the adventure (69 s, 2023); In search of gold (146 s, 2023; DOP Jonathan Weatherley, folk music);
  Shepherd (85 s, 2023); Into the backcountry (150 s, 2021, London to New Forest to Isle of Wight); The Cargo Range (120 s);
  A road less travelled #002 Sam Beer (musician portrait); Handmade in England (2016).
- Style: documentary adventure, natural light, British landscapes, folk and acoustic music, long form, 16:9.
- **Gap:** no short, macro-led, scroll-stopping product hero film. None of their films proves the waterproof suede on screen.

**Product truth (their own copy)**
- Marine Blue Suede collection launched **13 Nov 2026** ("Suede Collection 03", after Storm Grey 2023 and Tobacco Brown 2024).
- Waterproof Italian suede, **moss-backed** (resin under the hide) plus a top-layer water-resistant treatment. "Impermeable to moisture without compromising its character."
- Weekender: £1,875, 42 L, two waterproof shoe compartments, padded laptop sleeve.
- Gear Patrol (17 Nov 2026) frames the range for **winter and holiday travel**.
- What the bag actually looks like (from the product photos): navy suede body, **black** full-grain leather rolled twin handles
  with a black grip wrap and two brass rivets, black leather corner panels, black leather base with brass studs,
  gold brass zips with black leather pulls, brass hardware on black leather side tabs, faint debossed bird logo.
  (Correction: an earlier brief said "tan trim". That was wrong. The trim is black.)

**Niche benchmarks**
- Rimowa "Never Still" (2025, Rosé, Jay Chou, Lewis Hamilton; dents as character) and "For a Lifetime of Lives" (heirloom across generations).
  The category sells durability, heirloom and journey. Bennett Winch's Lifetime Promise and patina story sit in the same lane.
- Luxury reels that perform: macro material and light-play shots, ASMR mechanism sounds (zips, clasps), slow-motion texture, one surprise moment,
  a hook in the first second. Luxury guidance: high craft and low cadence. No trend gimmicks that cheapen.
- FOOH and CGI giant-product stunts (Jacquemus) go viral but would feel off-brand for quiet British luxury. We borrow only the
  "impossible physics" moment (frozen sea spray), executed with restraint.

## 2. Concept: MARINE

**Line:** *Marine Blue. Named for the sea. Built to meet it.*

The bag goes from the smoothest place in Britain (a tailor's street in London) to the roughest (a storm-lashed Atlantic cliff),
takes the full force of the sea, and arrives bone dry. That is "the rough with the smooth" and "built for a kicking",
told in 25 seconds, with one AI-only signature moment: **the sea frozen mid-air around the bag**.

End card (added in post, serif): **MARINE BLUE** / *Named for the sea. Built to meet it.* / **BENNETT WINCH** · Handmade in England

## 4. Production method
1. Keyframes: GPT Image 2.6 (high, 2K) with the real product photos as references, one still per shot, checked against the photos.
2. Motion: Sand AI MAGI-2 (1080p) image-to-video from each keyframe, with native audio for the sound effects.
3. Music: ElevenLabs Music v2.5, 25 s instrumental (cello, low strings, British folk undertone; a nod to their folk-scored films).
4. Edit: ffmpeg cut to the timings above, music mixed under the sound effects, end card burned in.
