## New format (in-thread, plain text, ~110 words)
1. "I had an idea for {their product} and wanted to ask before I make anything."
2. ONE specific film idea in 2–3 sentences, built from their own product, story or tagline.
3. "15 seconds, original music, and your real {bottle/piece/product} rebuilt in 3D so it's exact in every shot.
   It's free, and yours to post or not."
4. One proof link: The Beast, "for Maison Yusif, 2026 Fragrance Brand of the Year at the Ghana Beauty Awards".
5. "Shall I make it? A simple yes or no is all I need."
6. Price card line. No opt-out line.
Why: short; proves homework with an idea, not a compliment; nothing asked of them but one word; "no" is made easy,
which gets more answers than silence.
