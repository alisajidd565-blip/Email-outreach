## 4. FAQ (question max 150, answer max 300)

**Q: Do I really own the music?**
A: Yes. Every track is composed for your ad and delivered with full commercial rights. You own the final video and its music 100%, so you can post it, boost it, run it as a paid ad or put it on your website with no licensing fees.

**Q: Will my ad get muted or flagged on Instagram, TikTok or Meta Ads?**
A: Not because of the music. Muting and copyright claims come from unlicensed songs, and ours are original. Meta can still review ads for other reasons (claims, targeting), but the soundtrack won't be the problem.

**Q: What do you need from me?**
A: 3 to 6 clear photos of your product (phone photos are fine), your logo, and a few words about the mood you want. A product link and 1 to 2 ads you like help too.

**Q: Will my bottle and label look exactly like the real product?**
A: That's our priority. Product-accuracy revisions are unlimited: if the label, cap, colour or shape isn't right, we fix it until it matches.

**Q: Which aspect ratios do I get?**
A: All of them: 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories, 1:1 and 4:5 for feed, and 16:9 for YouTube and your website, all in 4K.

**Q: Can you handle ongoing marketing, not just one ad?**
A: Yes. Beyond one-off campaign ads we create monthly ad sets, ad creatives and product snippet shots. Message me for a package price.
