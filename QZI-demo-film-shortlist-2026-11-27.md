## Why each one, and a film idea

1. **Furlan Marri**: Swiss microbrand that won the GPHG 2021 Horological Revelation Prize for the Mechaquartz "Mr. Grey", only 8 months after its Kickstarter. The Disco Volante (38mm 1930s "flying saucer" case, hand-wound Peseux 7001, about CHF 2,500, colourways Havana, Verde, Celeste) is the hero.
   Film: a 1930s Geneva night, a flying saucer of light descends over Lake Geneva and lands as the Disco Volante case. The dial colour shifts Havana, Verde, Celeste. 15-20 s.
2. **Isle of Harris Distillery**: Isle of Harris Gin made with hand-harvested sugar kelp (gathered by a local diver), distilled in a still called "The Dottach", in an iconic ribbed bottle that ripples like the sea. Also The Hearach single malt; top bottles are £80-150.
   Film: underwater kelp forest, a diver's hand harvesting sugar kelp, the sea's ripples freezing into the ribbed glass, then a G&T on a Harris beach. 15-20 s.
3. **Bennett Winch**: founded 2014 by Robin Bennett and Robin Winch, handmade in England (Tuscan leather, London brass, Japanese zips). Weekenders run £1,750-2,550, and there's a 24H Le Mans collaboration line. Newest drop: the Marine Blue suede collection.
   Film: Le Mans at dusk, a classic car boot opens, the Marine Blue suede S.C Holdall in macro, the stitching and brass catching light. 15-20 s.
4. **Pump Street Chocolate**: father-and-daughter Chris and Joanna Brennan, started as a bakery in Orford in 2010. Bean-to-bar from single estates, King's Award 2023, many Academy of Chocolate awards. Sourdough & Sea Salt 66% is the signature bar, and gift boxes go up to £425.
   Film: sourdough crust cracking in slow motion, sea salt from the Suffolk coast, cacao pods, all melting into one Sourdough & Sea Salt bar in its pink wrapper. 15 s.
5. **MONC**: handcrafted frames from £300 (Alder, Kairo, Paragon, Valen at £460), stores on Chiltern Street and Monmouth Street. They already commission films and "In The Frame" portraits, so they clearly pay for visual content.
   Film: an acetate block milled and polished in macro, morphing into the Alder frame, then London streets reflected in the lenses. 15 s.
