## Before you send (5 min)

1. **Voiceover line.** Every email says "(voiceover too, if you want one)", which offers VO as part of the service without claiming the Lonéz ad has one. If the Mandarin & Sea Salt ad **does** have a voiceover, change it to "…added captions, music and a voiceover."
2. **3-day promise.** Your price sheet says 15s = 2 days and 30s = 4 days. "3 days" is true for a 15s ad, so quote 15s if they reply, or reword to "in 3–4 days."
3. **Check two claims yourself** on your phone before sending:
   - #7 Kymora: confirm instagram.com/kymorakandles really stops at Apr 2022 and that they haven't moved to a new handle.
   - #11 Waveland: confirm instagram.com/wavelandcandle really shows 0 posts. If not, cut that paragraph.
4. Send 3–4 a day from Gmail, spaced out, and log each one in the CSV as you go.
5. Follow-ups: use Follow-up 1 (day 3, offer a free 5-second test) and Follow-up 2 (day 7) from `Outreach_3C_Script.md`.
