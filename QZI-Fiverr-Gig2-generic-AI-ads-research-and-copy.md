## What the winners share
1. Title packs the search terms: "ai video ads", "ai commercial", "ai product ads". Under 80 chars.
2. Opening line names the buyer's fear (looks like AI, morphing, generic slop) and answers it.
3. Three tiers that step up by number of videos, length and orientations, not just price.
4. FAQ answers: does it look real, do I need a script, do I ship the product, which platforms, commercial use.
5. Reviews praise communication, speed, and "exceeded expectations". Message-before-order invites a chat.
6. Gallery: gig video first, many real work samples. Fiverr gives 1 video + 3 images + 2 PDFs here.

## Our angle
Realistic (product stays true, no warped logos), fully finished (edit, captions, music, sound), optional cameo (Ali's own face is in the ads), any category, priced above the $10 race but below the Pro tier ($80 / $120 / $200) because a new seller has 0 reviews.

**FAQ:**
1. Do I need a script? No. Send a brief or just your goal and I write the concept and script (Standard and Premium).

4. Which platforms? Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Meta ads and your website. Formats depend on the package.
5. Can you put me or a model in the ad? Yes, in Standard and Premium. Send 3 to 5 clear photos.
6. Can I use it for paid ads? Yes, full commercial use of the delivered ad.
