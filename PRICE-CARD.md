## Terms
- Revisions: 1 round (Snippet, Starter), 2 rounds (others). Storyboard/keyframe approval on Directed, Short Film, Signature. Extra round = 20% of package.
- Formats: master 9:16. Extra ratios (4:5, 1:1, 16:9) $35 each, free from Short Film up.
- Rights: perpetual worldwide commercial use (site, organic, paid digital, distributors/stockists). TV/cinema/outdoor quoted separately. QZI keeps portfolio rights. Music is original and royalty-free for the client.
- Delivery 3–5 working days; rush +30%.
- Quote in USD or EUR, never local currency.

## Why these numbers
- Market (2026): Fiverr AI ads $30–65 budget, $80–200 mid, $290–490+ premium; AI UGC $75–150 per 15–30 s;
  AI agencies ~€200/video or $1.5–5K/month retainers; traditional 30 s commercial $1.5K–50K.
- Credit cost per ad: ~$20–50 (15 s), $50–120 (30 s), $120–250 (60 s). The old $30–$500 card lost money on spec-heavy jobs.
- Too cheap is a credibility risk with brands selling €200+ bottles.
