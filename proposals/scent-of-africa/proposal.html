01The first film: Anancy

Free, no obligation. Yours to post whatever you decide afterwards.

Creative direction — "The Storyteller"

Anansi owns all the stories. This film tells one without words. A single golden thread, like spider silk, travels through the landscapes Scent of Africa is built on: roots and red earth, a river at dusk, the savannah horizon, a starry sky. Wherever the thread passes, the fragrance comes alive — a flash of cardamom and bergamot in the light; iris, creamy sandalwood and ambrette as the thread softens; cedar, vetiver and Somali frankincense as night falls. At the end the thread weaves itself into the silhouette of the Anancy bottle.

The bottle is the real one, composited from your product photographs, never redrawn. Mood: majestic, warm, confident yet gentle — strength and softness, as your own words put it. Palette: black, deep gold, dusk amber. An original score is composed for the film (a kora motif over a modern cinematic bed) and is royalty-free, so the film can run as a paid ad without copyright claims.

Duration and formats

  • Hero film, 20–25 seconds, 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories
  • A 10-second cut-down for Stories and paid placements
  • 1:1 and 16:9 versions of the hero
  • Optional voice-over, English (French available): one line from the legend

How it happens

  1. You send 4–6 phone photos of the bottle (front, back, cap, label) and your logo.
  2. Within 3 working days: a storyboard of 6–8 frames for your approval.
  3. Within 7 working days of approval: the finished film, two rounds of revisions included.
