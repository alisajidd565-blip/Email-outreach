01Why this matters

For a fragrance house, the most important asset today is its digital presence. People can't smell a perfume through a screen, so what they see is what makes them buy. The biggest investment is making sure your brand reaches as many people as possible, while keeping one clear identity that is only yours. That is what builds a lasting commercial advantage over other brands.

So this is not a budget for "ads". It is marketing that brings customers: a steady flow of films that put Scent of Africa in front of new people every day, in a look no one else has. Sixteen fragrances, the legends behind them, ingredients from across the continent and the Akua'ba launch in December give us stories for every day of the month.
