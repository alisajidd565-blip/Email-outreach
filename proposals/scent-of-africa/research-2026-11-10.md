### What this means for the pitch
- They are not a small indie. A 600-person manufacturer with major-house perfumers and Cannes presence has a marketing budget. Price at international, not Ghanaian-agency, levels — but stay far under traditional production.
- December is their launch month (Akua'ba in Accra). A 30-day plan that starts 1 Dec is a launch plan, not a generic retainer. That is the hook.
- 16 bottles + 2 collections + a travel-retail audience = enough stories for daily content for a year. Their feed shows launches and events, not daily product storytelling. That gap is the sale.
- Their style: cinematic, dark/gold, mythic, ingredient-led. The Beast / Atlas Original / El Patrón match it. Bad Influence is too playful for them. Lead with the darker films.
- They already have a brand film and professional photography. Our value is volume + consistency + story per bottle, not "you have no content".

### Beauty / fragrance social benchmarks (what good looks like)
- Beauty brands post ~5 Instagram posts/week (3 Reels, 1 static, 2 carousels) and ~8 TikToks/week (Dash Social 2026).
- Best-engaging beauty accounts are smaller, fragrance-and-personality-led (House of Sillage 1.8%, Scent Beauty 1.6%) — not mega-brands (apaya.com, citing Rival IQ / Dash Social).
- "Keep photos in the Instagram mix": beauty is the one vertical where stills still perform (Rival IQ). Supports Ali's instruction that lower plans carry more images.
- Recommended 2026 mix: 60–70% Reels, 20–30% carousels, ~10% single images (Socialinsider).
- Frequency raises reach, not engagement; consistency through launch season beats bursts (Quimby Q3 2026, apaya).

## 4. Pricing logic for the three plans
- Anchor on international studio retainers (MAW $3,000 for 4–8 assets; e-commerce video retainers $2,500–4,500 for far fewer pieces), not Ghana graphics packages.
- Lower plans: more stills and snippets (cheap to make, and stills still work in beauty). Top plan: a film a day.
- Per-bottle logic: 16 fragrances. Takeover ≈ 2 films per fragrance per month. Growth = one film for each 2026 launch (Akua'ba ×6, Anancy, Assaye) plus the four Eternal Legends bestsellers. Presence = four launch films + stills/snippets covering the rest of the range.
- Keep a clear jump between tiers so the middle one looks like the sensible choice, and the top one looks like the launch-month choice.
