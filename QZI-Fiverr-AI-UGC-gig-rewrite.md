## FAQ
- **Do I need to write a script?** No. Send talking points if you have them. If not, I write the hook, script and call to action.
- **Is this made with AI?** Yes. The footage is AI-generated, then edited, captioned and scored by me. Follow the disclosure rules of the platform you post on.
- **Will it look like my real product?** That is the priority. Send clear photos (front, side, label close-up) and I match them. I will flag anything that needs a better photo.
- **Can I use it for paid ads?** Yes. Commercial usage rights are included: 3 months on AI UGC Starter, unlimited on AI UGC Pro and AI Ad Pack.
- **What formats do you deliver?** 9:16 vertical by default. AI UGC Pro adds 16:9, and AI Ad Pack includes all formats (9:16, 16:9 and 1:1).

> Corrected 22 Sep when going live. Fiverr requires a "usage rights" value per package and locks Basic at 3 months, so an unlimited "commercial use" promise would have been false on Basic. The formats answer now matches the package descriptions (Standard is 9:16 + 16:9 only).
- **What if I want changes?** Revisions are included in every package.
