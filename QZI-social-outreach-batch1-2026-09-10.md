# QZI Labs — social outreach batch 1 (10 Sep 2026)

Brands where email sourcing failed today (contact-form-only sites). Reaching them via Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn instead.

**How to send:** open each profile in Chrome, send from your own account (not automated — IG/FB ban scripted-looking activity, and a real DM lands better). Paste the text exactly.
**Pace:** 10–15 DMs/day max, spread through the day, not back-to-back.
**First touch = no links.** If they reply interested, *then* send the two Drive portfolio links.
**If a handle doesn't resolve when you go to send, skip it.**
